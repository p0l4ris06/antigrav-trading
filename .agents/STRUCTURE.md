## The Two Live Paths

There are **two separate live execution daemons**:

| File | Exchange | Risk Guards |
|------|----------|-------------|
| `terminal.py` | Alpaca (crypto, long-only) | MAX_ORDER_NOTIONAL=$5,000 · MAX_DAILY_DD=5% (Phase 0 fix) |
| `live_daemon.py` | ccxt (Binance/Bybit/Kraken) | Full circuit breaker + drawdown guard already present |

## Name Collision Resolution

| Old name | What it was | Renamed to | Not to be confused with |
|----------|-------------|------------|------------------------|
| `core/gateway.py` | ccxt exchange adapter | `core/exchange_adapter.py` | `antigravity/gateway/server.py` (FastAPI WS) |
| `core/overseer.py` | Page-Hinkley drift detector | `core/drift_detector.py` | `antigravity/overseer/daemon.py` (agentic overseer) |
